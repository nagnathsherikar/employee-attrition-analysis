# Employee Attrition Analysis & Retention Insights

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.10%2B-blue) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green) ![Pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-2.x-150458)

Exploratory data analysis of employee attrition survey data to identify the key drivers behind employees leaving an organization, and translate those findings into actionable retention recommendations.

## Overview
Employee attrition is the rate at which employees leave an organization without their positions being refilled. High attrition raises turnover costs and hurts organizational continuity. This project explores an HR attrition dataset to understand which factors correlate most strongly with employees leaving.

## Objectives
- Explore the dataset and its features
- Understand relationships between variables and attrition
- Identify variables associated with higher attrition
- Translate findings into retention recommendations for HR teams

## Key Insights
- Employees working overtime are more likely to leave
- Low monthly income correlates with higher attrition
- Job role and years at company significantly impact attrition
- Distance from home, work-life balance, and job/environment satisfaction all show measurable effects on attrition

## Recommendations
- Offer work-life balance support and flexible arrangements
- Provide mentorship and clear career growth paths
- Ensure competitive, market-aligned compensation
- Foster a positive, inclusive work environment
- Run regular employee engagement surveys to catch issues early

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Conclusion
This analysis helps organizations understand the key drivers of employee attrition, supporting HR teams in designing more effective retention strategies.
